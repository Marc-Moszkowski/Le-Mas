Provençal Country Mas




Location


Located in Provence's Haut Var region, 32 km (20 mi) from the Mediterranean at a healthy elevation of close to 600 m (2,000 ft.)



Master House


Originally built XVIIth century, rebuilt to contemporary standards.

The house is built of stones with some walls over 90 cm (3 ft.) thick and has a roman tile roofing in pristine condition.

The total furnished surface area is 760 sq. m. (8,500 sq. ft.)



Estate


The estate surface area is 135 ha (330 acres) of wooded hills, primarily oak trees with some pinewoods, cypresses and cherry trees with three natural water springs.



Accommodations


  • 7 bedrooms with 6 bathrooms

  • 2 oversized living rooms

  • 1 dining room with a 5 m (16 ft) long oak table

  • 2 indoors and 1 outdoors fully equipped kitchens

  • 2 wine cellars

  • 4 terrasses or patios

  • Spring fed pool, 15 m (50 ft) by 6 m (20 ft)

  • Oversized central heating system.



Caretaker's quarters


Ancillary quarters are located in a separate house of approximately 200 sq.m (2,200 sq. ft.) ground surface area, complete with a 3 vehicles garage, three bedrooms, one living room, one kitchen and one bathroom plus a huge second floor enclosed storing area, truck accessible.
