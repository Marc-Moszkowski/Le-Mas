A perfect second home in Provence

For the Purest - Simply Provence, "Le Mas"

Le Mas: An enchanting place in an old world setting embedded in a hillside. The main home has approximately 8,500 square feet in 330 acres of private hills and forests. Features include 2 master suites and 5 additional bedrooms, 6 bathrooms, 2 indoor kitchens and 1 outdoor kitchen. A southern exposed spring fed swimming pool overlooks fruit trees, rolling hills and flowers. Le Mas is constructed of limestone, lava, hardwood beams, tile roof, tile flooring and features a wine cellar. Rare self-sustaining water source from the privately owned mountain spring makes this estate complete. Le Mas is furnished with old world furnishings and comes with a 2,200 square foot care-takers home.


A respite, the Cote d’Azur and its surrounding country side:


Le Mas, a private retreat in the country side awaits your arrival in Provence. Located sixty minutes from Nice. A fifty minute drive to Cannes, St. Tropez and the Verdon Gorge offers enough privacy for a rock star! If you are a sports driver and nature enthusiast, you will love this country estate . The Provence wineries are thirty minutes down the hill. Artisans and bakeries are right around the corner. Two major international ski resorts are a scenic 80 miles drive away and make skiing excursions a daily reality.


You will drive under a castle and wind around a village road to reach Le Mas.


Le Mas is located above a medieval village on a small gravel road. There is a special spot along the drive and just outside Le Mas where you can view the Mediterranean Sea. The landscape is enhanced with fruit trees, such as pear, cherry, plum and fig. Fragrant native flowers exude a sweet perfume scent. The roadside features thyme, rosemary and other herbs.

Location

Located in Provence's Haut Var region, 32 km (20 mi) from the Mediterranean at a healthy elevation of close to 600 m (2,000 ft.)


Master House

Originally built XVIIth century, rebuilt to contemporary standards.
The house is built of stones with walls over 90 cm (3 ft.) thick and has a roman tile roofing in pristine condition.
The total furnished surface area is 760 sq. m. (8,500 sq. ft.)


Estate

The estate surface area is 135 ha (330 acres) of wooded hills, primarily oak trees with some pinewoods, cypresses and cherry trees with three natural water springs.
